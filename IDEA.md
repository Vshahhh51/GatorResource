# GatorResource

An independent student-built prototype helping San Francisco State students find campus support from a plain-language description of their needs.

Students often have several needs at once: groceries, a job, rent, academic help, or wellbeing support. Gemini interprets those needs and constraints and selects up to three entries from a small, curated SFSU directory. Each match separates its AI explanation from an official-source next step and link. Unknown availability and eligibility stay explicit.

The directory is also browsable without AI. No accounts, uploads, database, live crawling, or university-system integration are part of the MVP. Search descriptions and results are temporary; provider data policies still apply. This is not an official SFSU service or emergency-response tool.

For a campus pilot, invite a small opt-in group of students to try synthetic scenarios, ask campus resource staff to review directory facts, and collect feedback about usefulness without collecting search histories. Measure whether students can identify an appropriate next step; review sources regularly before expanding coverage.

Hackathon positioning: SFSU students are beneficiaries; Gemini supplies multi-need interpretation; Cloud Run is the intended additional Google service. Actual deployment and use of credited billing must be verified separately. This is not a Gemma/open-weight entry.
